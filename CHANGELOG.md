## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-25

### Added
- **Scene Switcher**: Scene View toolbar overlay with a scene dropdown (Build Settings or all scenes in `Assets`),
  an *Auto enter Play Mode* toggle and a *Show all scenes* toggle. Settings are saved per project.
